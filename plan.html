The problem in 60 seconds

For each test row we get four text fields — benchmark, condition, subject_content (the AI being tested), item_content (the question) — and must return P(subject answers item correctly). Subjects are seen in training; items are new (item-side cold-start). Metric is mean log-likelihood of true labels under our predicted probabilities.

Stage 1 ↔ Stage 2 contract · pin this in the meeting

Stage 2 reads only two files from Stage 1: data/irt/b.pt (regression targets) and data/irt/item_to_id.json (alignment). Other Stage-1 outputs (theta.pt, log_a.pt, subject_to_id.json) are consumed only by the submission's model.py at inference time.

Parallel development pattern:

  1. Both teams agree on the schema (literally this section) in the meeting.
  2. Stage 2 dev runs python scripts/mock_irt.py once → populates data/irt/ with synthetic-but-correctly-shaped outputs. Builds + tests the full content-head + submission pipeline against mocks.
  3. Stage 1 dev fits real IRT, overwrites data/irt/. Schema unchanged → Stage 2 just re-runs train_content_head.py against real targets.
  4. Day 3: build submissions/v1_irt/ against real outputs from both stages.
  5. Stage 1 hyperparameter sweeps don't break Stage 2 — just require re-running train_content_head.py.

Breaking-change rule: renaming or reshaping b.pt or item_to_id.json requires both teams' explicit agreement. Going from 1PL to 2PL with joint (b, log_a) regression is a contract change — plan ahead.

Approach

Three model families layered onto the spec's PGE pipeline, plus an active-learning channel.

Stage 1 · IRT on the response matrix

Fit per-subject ability θ_s and per-item parameters (a_i, b_i) by MLE on the training response matrix. Produces the targets for Stage 2a and the lookup table for Stage 2b.

Stage 2a · Content head (item text → IRT params)

Frozen all-mpnet-base-v2 sentence encoder + small MLP regressing onto Stage 1's (b̂, log â). The only stage that must generalize at test time, since every test item is new.

Stage 2b · Subject lookup

Subjects are not cold. Look up θ̂_s from Stage 1 by normalized name (extracted from the Name: line of subject_content). Population-mean fallback for unrecognized names.

Stage 3 · Combine

P(correct) = σ(â · (θ̂ − b̂)). Cast to native Python float before returning.

Adaptive labeling · diversity acquisition + online Platt

Ship labeling.py with k-means diversity (or online farthest-point sampling). The 25 revealed labels per round (K=5 × 5 categories) feed a one-parameter Platt calibration fit at the first predict() call of the round and cached on a module variable.

Optional · Local LLM-as-judge ensemble

7B-class instruction model declared in models.txt. Read next-token p(yes) / (p(yes) + p(no)) under a fixed prompt. Log-mixture with the IRT-content predictor, weights tuned on validation. Adds compute cost; gates on time and Modal budget.
